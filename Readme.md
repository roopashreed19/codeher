Project Status
Completed Features
Question Generation by Difficulty
Students can upload their study material or textbook and receive automatically generated subjective questions categorized into Easy, Medium, and Hard levels. This helps them practice based on their comfort zone and gradually build up.

AI Chatbot for Doubt Solving
An intelligent chatbot that answers subject-specific questions from the uploaded content, offering students 24x7 support similar to a personal tutor.

Performance Analysis
Students can input their marks, and the platform automatically calculates their overall percentage and identifies their weakest subject based on performance. This gives them actionable insights into what areas need improvement.

In Progress / Upcoming Features
Mock Test Generation
Students will be able to generate and take full-length mock tests based on the previously generated questions, simulating real exam environments.

Dopamine Reels Section
A unique section to keep students engaged through short, motivational, and productivity-focused reels — providing quick dopamine boosts without the distraction of traditional social media.
